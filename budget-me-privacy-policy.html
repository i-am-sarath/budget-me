Privacy Policy

Budget Me — AI-Powered Budget Tracker  ·  Last updated: May 5, 2026

Overview

Budget Me ("the App") is an AI-powered personal budget tracker. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and what we do not do with it. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

The short version: We only access your camera and microphone to let you scan receipts and enter expenses by voice. We do not sell, store, or share your personal data with third parties — except for AI processing via OpenAI, which is strictly used to power in-app features.

Permissions We Request

The App requests the following device permissions:

  • CAMERA — Used exclusively for scanning receipts and bills. Images captured are processed locally or sent to OpenAI for text extraction to auto-fill expense fields. Photos are not stored on our servers.
  • RECORD_AUDIO — Used exclusively for voice input, allowing you to add expenses hands-free. Audio is transcribed via OpenAI's API in real time and immediately discarded. No audio recordings are stored.

Data We Collect

The App does not collect personally identifiable information unless explicitly provided by you. The following summarizes what is processed:

  • Budget & expense data — Stored locally on your device only. We do not upload or sync your financial records to any server.
  • Camera images — Captured temporarily for receipt scanning. Sent to OpenAI only for text extraction and not retained afterward.
  • Voice audio — Captured temporarily for speech-to-text. Sent to OpenAI only for transcription and not retained afterward.
  • AI prompts & responses — Text queries you make to the AI assistant are processed by OpenAI. We do not log these interactions on our own servers.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service that receives any form of your data is OpenAI, used solely to power AI features (receipt parsing, voice transcription, budget insights). Data sent to OpenAI is governed by OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any advertising networks, analytics SDKs that track individuals, or data brokers. No personal or financial data is sold or shared with any other third party.

Data Retention & Security

Your budget and expense data lives entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. We do not maintain any backend database of user data. Camera and audio inputs are transient — they exist only for the duration of the AI processing request.

We recommend keeping your device and the App updated to ensure you benefit from the latest security improvements.

Children's Privacy

Budget Me is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
